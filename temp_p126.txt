The interpretation model is derived from the one-dimensional advection-dispersion equation (ADE): ∂C/∂t + v·∂C/∂x = D·∂²C/∂x², where v = 4Q/(πd²) is the mean flow velocity (cm/min) and D = α·v is the longitudinal dispersion coefficient (cm²/min), with Q the effective oil-phase volumetric flow rate (cm³/min), d the tubing inner diameter (cm), and α the longitudinal dispersivity (cm). The tracer pulse released from ESP-T into the oil phase during the shut-in period is described by the classical ADE analytical solution for an instantaneous slug injection, which yields the Gaussian-form rising component in Eq. (2). The sustained slow release of residual tracer from the epoxy matrix after the main pulse is captured by solving the ADE with a continuous-source boundary condition, yielding the erfc tailing component. The two regimes are blended via a hyperbolic tangent weighting function, producing a smooth, physically continuous breakthrough curve.
