During steady-state production, tracer concentrations are inherently low, and the production curve conveys information primarily through the magnitude and temporal trend of the concentration signal. To quantify the single-interval oil flow rate, tracer flux (FO) was introduced. FO is defined as the mass of tracer passing through the wellhead sampling point per unit time, which equals the tracer release rate of ESP-T. Figure 3-9(b) shows the variation in FO: it increases with OWR but is independent of total two-phase flow rate. At a constant OWR, the contact area between ESP-T and the oil phase remains unchanged, so FO stays constant regardless of total flow rate. Conversely, increasing OWR expands the oil-phase contact area with ESP-T, leading to higher FO.
